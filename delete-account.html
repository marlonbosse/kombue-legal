Delete account and data

Last updated: 17 November 2026

How to delete your account

Email mobdev@tuta.com from the address of your account with the subject "Delete Kombue account". We delete your account and personal data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account, email address, display name and household membership. Content shared in a household (recipes, lists, plan) remains for the other members until the household is deleted; on request we delete the whole household.

Local data

Uninstall the app or use Android settings → Apps → Kombue → Storage → Clear data.
