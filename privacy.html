What Kombue stores

Your email address (sign-in by email link or Google account), your display name, your household and its members, plus recipes, weekly plan, shopping lists, pantry and purchase history. All members of a household can see that household's content.

Where data is stored

Data is stored on a server we operate in a Hetzner data centre in the European Union (self-hosted Supabase). Transmission is encrypted.

Service providers

Sign-in emails are sent by Resend. Sign-in with Google is processed by Google under its own privacy policy. To prepare recipes, the server uses Google Gemini; only recipe text from public sources is processed, no personal data. Installation and updates go through Google Play.

What does not happen

No advertising, no tracking SDKs, no sharing for advertising. For shared shopping the app connects phones directly (Nearby Connections).

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification and deletion, and can request account deletion, see Delete account. You may complain to a data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 16.
